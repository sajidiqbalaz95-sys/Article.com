How to Start Learning Any New Skill Online

Learning ·

Pick one skill and stay with it for 30 days. Decide a small daily goal, such as 30 minutes of practice, and write down what you finished each day.

Use free tutorials first, then build a small project. Projects teach faster than watching videos, because mistakes show you what to learn next.

Review your progress every week and adjust. Consistency matters more than speed.

5 Habits for Safer Internet Use

Technology ·

Use a different strong password for every important account. A password manager makes this easy.

Turn on two-step verification where possible, keep your phone and apps updated, avoid unknown links, and check the web address before entering any details.

Saving Money: A Beginner's Guide

Money ·

Start by tracking where your money goes for one month. Most people find small expenses that add up.

Set aside a fixed amount as soon as you receive income, even if it is small. Build an emergency fund first, then plan bigger goals.
